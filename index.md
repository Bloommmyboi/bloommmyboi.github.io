## ✨ What Is PRISM?

PRISM is a video generation tool for humanoid motion. It focuses on two ideas:

- **Counterfactual video generation:** creating realistic variations of a motion video. For example, you can explore how a humanoid movement might look with a different object, direction, or action.
- **Humanoid loco-manipulation:** movement that combines walking and balance with reaching, carrying, pushing, or handling objects.

PRISM is designed to make these ideas easier to use through a friendly Windows workflow. Instead of building complex technical setups, you can open the app, choose a project, preview motion results, and export video files.
